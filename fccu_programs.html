With a focus on research and development, the Postgraduate programs at FCCU are taught by faculty of international repute. State-of-the-art laboratories and research facilities, seminars, symposia and workshops supplement the academic programs. FCCU offers a unique experience to postgraduate students, including the opportunity to conduct research with leading academics and with some of the very academic facilities.
